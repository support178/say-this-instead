Common questions

What should I say when my toddler refuses to get dressed?
Say calmly: "I can see you don't want to get dressed. It's time to get ready now. Do you want to put on your shirt first or your pants first?" Offering a small choice gives them a sense of control, which reduces resistance significantly.
Why does my toddler refuse to get dressed every morning?
Getting dressed involves a loss of autonomy — you're deciding what happens to their body. Toddlers resist this naturally. Small choices within the task (shirt or pants first, which shoes) dramatically reduce the battle because they regain some control.
How do I get my toddler dressed when they keep running away?
Don't chase — it becomes a game. Instead, get down to their level, make eye contact, give a simple choice, and wait. If they don't choose, guide them calmly into the first step. Staying calm and moving slowly is faster than rushing.
Is it normal for toddlers to refuse to get dressed?
Completely normal. Dressing involves physical contact they didn't initiate, transitions away from play, and decisions being made for them — all things toddlers naturally push back on. Consistent, calm routines with small choices built in are what work over time.
