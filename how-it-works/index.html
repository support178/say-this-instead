What you actually get

Scripts that work in real life

Here's an example, this one is for hitting.

🗣 Say this
"I won't let you hit. Hitting hurts."
⏱ What to do
1
Block or gently hold their hands
2
Stay calm and firm.
3
Keep your body between them and others if needed
4
Reconnect warmly once they're calm
⚠️ Avoid
❌Yelling or hitting back
❌Long explanations in the heat of the moment
❌Ignoring it completely
🔁 If they do it again
"Hands are not for hitting. If you're angry, you can stomp your feet or say 'I'm mad'."
→ Stay close, speak quietly, keep your body relaxed
💡 Pro tip
"Stay physically calm and neutral. Your reaction teaches what's acceptable."

Every scenario follows the same structure so it's fast to read even when you're in the middle of it. The script is short by design. Toddlers can't process long explanations when they're dysregulated, and neither can you.

Add it to your home screen

Works like an app. Installs like one too

There's nothing to download from an app store. Add it directly to your home screen in about 10 seconds then it works exactly like an app, including fully offline.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

  • 1
    Open say-this-instead.com in Safari
  • 2
    Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen
  • 3
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  • 4
    Tap Add

Must use Safari. Chrome on iPhone won't offer the install option.

🤖 Android

  • 1
    Open say-this-instead.com in Chrome
  • 2
    Tap the three dot menu in the top right corner
  • 3
    Tap Add to Home Screen or Install App
  • 4
    Tap Install

Chrome may prompt you automatically after a couple of visits.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free?▾
7 scenarios are completely free. A subscription ($4.99/month or $29.99/year) unlocks all 30+ scripts, lets you save favourites for quick access, and includes new scripts regularly. There's a 7-day free trial.
What age is it for?▾
The scripts are written for parents of toddlers aged roughly 1 to 4 years old. Many of the approaches (calm boundaries, simple language, giving limited choices) also work well with older children, but the situations and language are aimed squarely at the toddler years.
Does it work offline?▾
Yes. Once you've visited the site, everything is stored on your device. Install it to your home screen and it works even in aeroplane mode. This was a deliberate decision: you need it in the moment, not when you have perfect internet.
Who wrote the scripts?▾
The scripts are grounded in evidence-based approaches to toddler behaviour, drawing on child development research, attachment theory and positive discipline principles. They're written to be practical first: real words you can actually say under pressure, not textbook theory you have to translate in the moment.
Do I need to create an account?▾
No account needed for free access, just open the app and use it. If you subscribe, you'll receive a unique access code by email which you enter once to unlock everything. No username, no password to forget.
How do I cancel my subscription?▾
You can cancel any time through Stripe, which handles all billing. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
Will there be more scripts?▾
Yes, new scenarios are added regularly for premium subscribers. If there's a situation you'd love a script for, get in touch and suggest it.
How do I get in touch?▾
Email us at support@say-this-instead.com for help with your subscription, access code issues, or to suggest a scenario you'd like to see covered.
